02

About

I earned my PhD in Technology from Purdue University in 2026. For my dissertation, I created GeoBotsVR, a learning environment where beginners build, repair, and operate a robot. I used it to investigate how assistance during practice affects learning and transfer to a physical task.

At Illinois, I compare forecasts from large language models with results from human-participant XR studies. My earlier work includes VR haptics and human–robot collaboration, as well as immersive prototypes at Dolby Laboratories and Delta Air Lines. Across these projects, I combine system development, participant studies, and quantitative analysis.
