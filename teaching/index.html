Teaching experience

Purdue University · Fall 2021 and August 2024–December 2025

CNIT 25501 · Introduction to Object-Oriented Programming

As a graduate teaching assistant, I led weekly Java labs, helped develop coding exercises, and supported more than 60 students through labs, office hours, and individual assistance. I graded programming projects and gave feedback on code, design decisions, and problem-solving approaches.

02

Mentoring and teaching approach

I have mentored undergraduate students in computer science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and in industrial engineering at Texas Tech University.

My GeoBotsVR research shapes how I design practice and assessment. I start with examples and structured tasks, then give students more control over their approach. I ask students to explain their choices and apply the same ideas under changed conditions, including individual follow-up tasks after team projects.

I also draw on my industry work at Delta Air Lines and Dolby Laboratories to help students translate requirements into prototypes, test the resulting system, and document decisions others can use.

03

Proposed course designs

Proposed · IE 38600

Work Analysis and Design I

Students would measure a work system, compare design alternatives, and test a redesign while considering errors, physical demands, accessibility, and cost. A team report and individual explanation would document their decisions.

Proposed · IE 57700

Human Factors in Engineering

Students would connect human factors concepts with an evaluation of an interactive system. Projects would define a performance question, select suitable measures, and turn findings into design decisions.
